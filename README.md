## 🎬 Favorite Movie: *The Book of Life*

**The Book of Life** (2014) is a colorful animated film inspired by the Mexican holiday Día de los Muertos (Day of the Dead).

### Why I Love It
- Beautiful, vibrant animation
- Great music and fun cover songs
- A heartwarming story about love, family, and being true to yourself

### Main Characters
| Character | Role |
|-----------|------|
| Manolo | A bullfighter who dreams of being a musician |
| Joaquín | A brave soldier and Manolo's best friend |
| María | A strong, smart, and kind-hearted girl |

> "Follow your heart" is the big message of the movie.

## 📺 Favorite Series: *Modern Family*

**Modern Family** is a comedy series about three different families who are all connected.

### Why I Love It
1. It's hilarious and relatable
2. The characters are lovable and memorable
3. It shows that every family is different, and that's okay

### The Families
- **The Pritchetts** – Jay and Gloria
- **The Dunphys** – Phil, Claire, and their kids
- **Mitchell and Cameron** – and their daughter Lily
